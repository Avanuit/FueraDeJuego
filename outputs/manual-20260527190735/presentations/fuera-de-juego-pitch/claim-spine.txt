PROJECT: Fuera de Juego (Pitch Deck)
OBJECTIVE: Sell the project as a high-impact transmedia experience.
CORE CLAIM: 'Fuera de Juego' isn't just a story; it's a digital ecosystem that turns passive readers into active witnesses of the human cost of the 2026 World Cup.

NARRATIVE ARC:
1. The Problem (The Silence): The World Cup sells a global party; the reality is gentrification and displacement.
2. The Solution (The Bridge): A transmedia bridge between an intimate graphic novel and an expansive, data-driven immersive web experience.
3. Product 1 - The Comic: Emotional entry point. Humanizing the data through three lives (Lucía, Marcus, Aiyana).
4. Product 2 - The Web Experience: The evidence room. Interactive maps, real testimonials, and documented displacement.
5. The Loop (The Hook): The 'Unlocked Chapter 4' – the user's testimony is the key to the ending.
6. The Impact: Projected reach and the call to invest in the voices the World Cup doesn't broadcast.
